SavComponents

Privacy

An account is optional: the components and the documentation work without one. It only lets you keep favourite components, saved configurations and your progress in the Academy.

What is stored

Your name, your email address, your password (only as a one-way hash), when you created the account and last used it, your favourite components, and the themes and components you save. Nothing else, and nothing is sold or shared.

SavComponents Academy (savcomponents.com/academy, also on Google Play) keeps, for a signed-in account, your learning progress (lessons, quiz scores, challenges, days of practice, badges, XP and level). If you choose a username and join the leaderboard, that username with your level, XP and day streak is shown to everybody; nothing else of you is. You can leave the leaderboard or clear the username at any time from the Academy profile.

The accounts are kept on the server of saveriomorelli.com, by the same author; savcomponents.com itself stores nothing about you.

Cookies and local storage

There are no cookies, analytics or advertising. When you sign in, a random sign-in key is kept in your browser's local storage for up to 30 days and sent only to the accounts server; signing out deletes it. The customiser also remembers your choices there, and the Academy keeps your progress and the code of your challenges there (on this device; in your account only when you sign in).

Theme links

A saved theme has a public address (its CSS) so you can use it on your own sites. Anyone who knows that address can read the theme, but not your name or email.

Emails

You only receive the link to confirm your address and, if you ask for it, the link to choose a new password.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from the account page: everything listed above is removed at once. Accounts whose email is never confirmed are deleted after 7 days. The administrator of the site can also disable or delete accounts.
