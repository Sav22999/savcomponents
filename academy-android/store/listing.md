# SavComponents Academy — Google Play listing

**App name:** SavComponents Academy

**Short description (80 characters at most):**
Learn web components by doing: lessons, quizzes, live challenges and badges.

**Full description:**
SavComponents Academy teaches you SavComponents, the library of ready-made web components (buttons, forms, dialogs, charts and
80 more) that work in any page with two files and no build step.

• A lesson for every component: what it is for, a live example, a playground to change its attributes and see the code,
  and how it stays accessible.
• Quizzes after every lesson, and a daily quiz with six new questions every day.
• Hands-on challenges: write real HTML and the app checks it live, step by step, from your first button to a whole header.
• XP, levels, day streaks, 30 badges and a collection of component cards to unlock.
• A leaderboard you can join with a username, and nothing else of you on show.
• The five SavComponents styles (Soft, Tilt, Sharp, Brutal, Glass) in light and dark mode.
• Sign in with your SavComponents account to keep your progress on every device; it also works without an account.
